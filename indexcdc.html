Disease Information Portal

The Disease Information Portal provides up-to-date information on Hopsitis Epidemica activity across the nation. Our surveillance efforts monitor the spread of Hopsitis Epidemica viruses, the impact on public health, and the effectiveness of prevention measures. This information is vital for healthcare providers, public health officials, and the general public to make informed decisions. The dashboard below visualizes the current state of the spread, showing geographic infection hotspots, demographic breakdowns by gender and hop strain, a trend of deaths, and detailed case breakdowns.
